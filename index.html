Data transparency

Live results and published contact fields come from OpenStreetMap contributors via Nominatim and Overpass. Research links open external search results for manual owner/contact verification; the app does not automatically crawl those sites or bypass social-platform restrictions. Coverage and contact fields vary by region. A lead score reflects listing completeness, not verified purchase intent. Email addresses are not fabricated, and the app does not send unsolicited emails. Respect source usage limits and applicable outreach rules. Data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
